# Code of Conduct

ULTRON is a small personal project shared publicly. Contributors and users
are expected to act like adults:

- **Be direct, not unkind.** Sharp technical feedback is welcome; insults are
  not. Critique the code, not the person.
- **Assume incompetence over malice.** Most bugs and bad commits come from
  someone learning, not someone trying to break your day.
- **No harassment.** Sexual harassment, sustained personal attacks, doxxing,
  or threats are immediate grounds for being blocked from the repo.
- **Stay on topic.** ULTRON is a developer tool. Political, religious, or
  off-topic crusades belong elsewhere.
- **English or Spanish are both fine** in issues, PRs, and comments.

## Enforcement

The repository owner (currently [@SkiTemplar](https://github.com/SkiTemplar))
moderates. Decisions are final but explained.

If you witness or experience behaviour that violates this code, open a
private GitHub Security Advisory (see `SECURITY.md`) or email the contact
listed in the repo profile. Reports are confidential.
